About Me
Engineer. Explorer.
Builder of Systems.

I'm Abdurasul — a backend engineer and researcher originally from Uzbekistan, now living and working in South Korea. My path wasn't accidental: I built it step by step, from a call centre operator at Mobiuz to a Research Engineer at IVIS, designing distributed systems for autonomous mobility platforms.

I don't just write code — I architect systems: real-time telemetry pipelines, MQTT-based data ingestion, fleet management dashboards, simulators, and database layers designed for high-throughput workloads. I bring a rare combination of technology, business thinking, and international experience — three degrees across IT, MBA, and IT-Business from universities in Uzbekistan and South Korea.

Emigration reshaped me. Moving to Korea meant losing familiar networks, facing language barriers, and learning to thrive in a completely different culture. It made me quieter but more focused, more selective but more intentional. I invest in myself, I tolerate discomfort, and I think years ahead. Currently preparing for a PhD abroad (Canada/Australia) with a GRE score of 316.

Outside of work, I'm all about movement: traveling, hiking, snorkeling, exploring new places. From Bali's cliffs to Dokdo's shores, from Japanese temples to Korean mud festivals — I believe the best engineering happens when you experience the world beyond your screen.
